# Grade 5 Mathematics Resource Library (sample)

These are the resources the campus has on hand for grade 5 math. The generator
will only name materials that appear in a file like this one, so keep it
current.

## Manipulatives available in every grade 5 classroom

- Fraction tiles (halves through twelfths), one set per pair of students
- Fraction circles, two class sets shared across the grade level
- Number line strips, 0-2 marked in halves, thirds, fourths, sixths, and eighths
- Base-ten blocks and place-value disks
- Unit cubes and rectangular prism nets for volume work
- Geoboards and rubber bands

## Adopted resources

- Adopted textbook, Grade 5, Chapters 1-11. Each chapter opens with a
  "Are You Ready?" diagnostic that maps to the prior grade's prerequisites.
- Digital practice platform, assigned by standard code. Teachers can build a
  practice set from any list of TEKS codes.
- Weekly spiral review packet, one page per week, released Monday.

## Routines the campus uses

- **Number Talk** opens every math block, 8 minutes, whole group. Students share
  strategies before any procedure is named.
- **Three Reads** protocol for word problems: read once for context, once for the
  question, once for the quantities.
- **Show Me** exit routine: students hold up a whiteboard with their answer and
  one sentence of reasoning.
- **Math Workshop** rotations: teacher table, independent practice, digital
  practice. Rotations run 15 minutes each.

## Sentence stems posted in every room

- "I know ___ because ___."
- "My model shows ___ , which means ___."
- "I agree with ___ , and I would add ___."
- "The remainder means ___ in this problem because ___."

## Intervention materials on the shelf

- Prerequisite skill packets for grades 3 and 4, organized by strand
- Fluency probes, one page each, timed at 3 minutes
- Small-group lesson cards for fractions, place value, and multiplication
- Family practice sheets available in English and Spanish

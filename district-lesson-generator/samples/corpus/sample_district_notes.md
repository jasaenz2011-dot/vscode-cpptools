# About this sample corpus

**These files are illustrative samples, not an official standards export.**
The standard codes follow the shape a Texas district would recognise, and the
descriptions are written to read like a scope and sequence, but they have not
been checked against the official published standards and must not be used to
plan real instruction.

Before using this system with students, replace every file in `corpus/` with
your district's own documents:

- your published scope and sequence or year-at-a-glance
- your official standards export (a CSV with a code column and a text column is
  ideal; a PDF works too)
- your resource library, campus routines, and intervention menu
- anything else a new teacher on your campus would be handed

Then run `dlg ingest` again. The system cites the file and row it took every
statement from, so it is easy to confirm that what it produced came from your
documents and not from these samples.

# Campus instructional expectations (sample)

Every math block is 60 minutes and follows the same frame:

- 8 minutes: Number Talk
- 35 minutes: core lesson, gradual release
- 15 minutes: small group or workshop rotations
- 2 minutes: exit ticket

Lessons are expected to name the standard in student-facing language, include at
least one check for understanding before independent practice, and end with a
piece of student work the teacher can sort into three piles.

Intervention is scheduled during the 30-minute daily WIN block ("What I Need").
Tier 2 groups meet three times a week; Tier 3 groups meet daily. Progress
monitoring probes are given every other Friday and entered into the campus
tracker by the following Monday.

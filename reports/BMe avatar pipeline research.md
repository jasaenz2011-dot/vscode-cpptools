# Four clean views beat one 8K master

**Bottom line.** The stitched, region-refined 4K+ master image is **not worth producing as converter input** for any of the four routes examined, and for the local open models it is provably pointless: Hunyuan3D‑2.x and TRELLIS downsample the subject to a **518×518** DINOv2 input and TRELLIS.2 to **512 + 1024**, all read from source code, so anything finer than roughly 440–1024 px across the robot's long axis is discarded before geometry is generated ([preprocessors.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2/main/hy3dgen/shapegen/preprocessors.py); [trellis2_image_to_3d.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2/main/trellis2/pipelines/trellis2_image_to_3d.py)). Hosted Tripo H3.1/P1, Meshy 7.1 and 3D AI Studio's Hunyuan 3.x publish no conditioning resolution at all, cap uploads at **20 MB** (Tripo, Meshy web) or **50 MB** (3D AI Studio), and their own guidance tops out at "1024 px or higher" ([Tripo docs, snippet](https://platform.tripo3d.ai/docs/generation); [Meshy help, snippet](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/15723519-how-to-get-better-image-to-3d-results-in-meshy); [3D AI Studio troubleshooting, snippet](https://docs.3daistudio.com/troubleshooting)), so for them the verdict is **Unverified but strongly Inferred: no**. The one lever every vendor documents for putting more information into the model is **multiple views** (Tripo 2–4, Meshy 1–4, Hunyuan 3.1 hosted up to 8, Hunyuan3D‑2mv 4), which is where a high-resolution master earns its keep: as the source from which tight ~1K–2K orthographic crops are cut, never as a single stitched sheet. The practical pipeline that the evidence supports is a hybrid: a cloud or local reconstruction of one master body from a material-neutral turnaround, segmentation into the pre-decided part list, then conventional Blender retopology, UV, bake, rig and Babylon.js 9.30.0 export, where frosted translucency should be authored opaque-first because Babylon's glTF transmission path renders a second **1024² MSAA‑4 half-float** opaque pass by default ([transmissionHelper.ts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BabylonJS/Babylon.js/master/packages/dev/loaders/src/glTF/2.0/Extensions/transmissionHelper.ts)). The local-first route on the RTX 5070 Ti is realistic for **shape only**; PBR texturing is blocked by an uncompiled `custom_rasterizer` for sm_120 on Windows (Hunyuan3D‑Paint) and by community-only 16 GB fit reports (TRELLIS.2). Mixar's headline claims are structurally true but its agent, tool prompts, retopology, UV, rigging and generation all run on a closed hosted backend that bills credits regardless of BYOK.

**How this report was produced and what it rests on.** Research subagents were used: four topic researchers (modular character workflows, Mixar fact-check, Babylon.js export and performance, converter limits), with the converter topic split into two researchers (Tripo/Meshy and 3D AI Studio/local models), plus a video researcher and one reviewer who reconciled conflicts and re-verified claims against primary sources on 2026‑10‑10. **Nothing was installed, run or tested in this session, and no video was watched and no transcript, description or publish date was read**; the evidence labels used throughout are therefore limited to **Documented**, **Observed in a demonstration**, **Inferred** and **Unverified**, and "Tested" never appears. Many vendor sites (tripo3d.ai, meshy.ai, 3daistudio.com, mixar.app, docs.comfy.org, huggingface.co, arxiv.org, youtube.com, doc.babylonjs.com, docs.blender.org) were unreachable from the research sandbox, so vendor facts rest on search-engine excerpts of the named pages and are marked **"Documented (indexed snippet; page not opened)"**; GitHub source files, licences and tags were read directly and those constants were spot re-verified by the reviewer. Every date below is 2026‑10‑10 unless stated. ## 1. Outcome first: is the stitched, region-refined 4K+ master image worth producing?

The question splits cleanly into what the open models do (readable from code) and what the hosted products do (undocumented). The open-model answer is a flat no for geometry and texture alike; the hosted answer is "no vendor says yes, every vendor's own guidance stops at 1–2K, and the only documented way to deliver more detail is more views." The master is justified only as a *source asset* from which per-view crops and a material-neutral variant are derived, and as a reference for your own bake or projection in Blender after reconstruction.

**Tripo (H3.1 `v3.1-20260211`, P1 `P1-20260311`, API and Studio web app).** Verdict: **not worth it; Inferred from the absence of any documented benefit plus the open-model lineage.** Tripo's API pages state only "JPEG and PNG … maximum size of 20MB … suggested resolution should be more than 256 x 256px" and publish no maximum pixel dimension (Documented, indexed snippet; [platform.tripo3d.ai/docs/generation](https://platform.tripo3d.ai/docs/generation)); the only hard pixel range anywhere is the **[20, 6000] px per side** rule on Alibaba Cloud Model Studio's hosting of Tripo‑H3.1/P1.0, which is a partner gateway and not Tripo's own API (Partner-documented, snippet; [help.aliyun.com](https://help.aliyun.com/zh/model-studio/tripo-3d-generation-api-reference)). Tripo's help centre says "aim for at least 1024x1024" and a marketing tutorial says "preferably 2048×2048 pixels or higher" ([help](https://www.tripo3d.ai/help/features/how-to-get-better-image-to-3d-results); [tutorial](https://www.tripo3d.ai/tutorials/tripo-ai-best-image-types)); the tutorial sentence is **Unverified vendor marketing** with no test behind it and contradicts the encoder analysis of VAST's open models, where TripoSR conditions at **512²** ([TripoSR config](https://github.com/MrForExample/ComfyUI-3D-Pack/blob/9e8096e50c5bcf35e1f3e34c6ae06216101f8a11/Configs/TripoSR_config.yaml)) and TripoSG's shipped DINOv2‑L preprocessor resizes the short edge to 256 and centre-crops **224²** (third-party code notes reading the HF weights; primary config not reached; [QtMeshEditor notes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fernandotonon/QtMeshEditor/800a36c802f98392655c6d98408a6b390a8e8a63/docs/TRIPOSG_EXPORT_NOTES.md)). Hosted H3.1 conditioning is **not publicly verified** and must not be inferred from these open models. What Tripo does document is multi-view: front view mandatory, at least two and up to four views, same 20 MB / >256 px rules (Documented, snippet; [developers.tripo3d.com](https://developers.tripo3d.com/en/docs/generation-multiview-to-model/standard.md)), and texture output tiers 2K/4K/8K announced August 2026 with "native 8192×8192 BaseColor" (company PR; [marketersmedia](https://news.marketersmedia.com/tripo-launches-8k-texture-to-advance-production-ready-ai-3d-asset-creation/89201029)) — the 8K texture is *generated*, not sampled from the input, so input detail above the texture model's unknown conditioning resolution does not transfer 1:1 (Inferred).

**3D AI Studio (hosted Hunyuan 3.0/3.1 Pro and Rapid, TRELLIS.2‑4B, Pixal3D, Tripo as "Prism", Deemos as "Forge", Hitem3D, Meshy).** Verdict: **not worth it; Unverified for the platform itself, Inferred from the models it wraps.** Public docs give only soft guidance: PNG/JPG/JPEG(/WebP), **under 50 MB**, "at least 1024x1024 recommended", with no maximum dimension, aspect rule or server-side resize statement (Documented, snippet; [troubleshooting](https://docs.3daistudio.com/troubleshooting); [FAQ](https://docs.3daistudio.com/faq)). The platform's Hunyuan is Tencent's **hosted-only 3.0/3.1** (a 3.5 listing also exists), whose existence is supported by three independent indications — the platform's API docs, a Replicate listing and model slugs `hunyuan_pro_v3`/`hunyuan_pro_v3.1` in Mixar's client code — but for which **no open weights exist**, so nothing in the Hunyuan3D‑2.x code tells you what 3D AI Studio's Hunyuan conditions on ([API doc, snippet](https://www.3daistudio.com/Platform/API/Documentation/3d-generation/tencent-hunyuan); [Replicate](https://replicate.com/tencent/hunyuan-3d-3.1)). Its "up to 4K" versus "up to 8K" texture claims conflict across its own blog pages and are vendor marketing ([blog A](https://www.3daistudio.com/blog/trellis-2-vs-hunyuan-3d-differences-explained); [blog B](https://www.3daistudio.com/blog/pixal3d-vs-trellis-2-vs-hunyuan-3d-comparison)). The platform's distinctive, documented lever is view count: Hunyuan 3.1 Pro accepts a `multi_view_images` array with **front mandatory and up to eight views** (front, left, right, back, top, bottom, left_front, right_front) for +20 credits (Documented, snippet; same API doc). For the TRELLIS.2 and Pixal3D models it hosts, the open code says the input is downscaled to ≤1024 px on the long side before encoding, so an 8K upload there is a wasted upload (Inferred from the open code; whether the hosted wrapper changes preprocessing is not publicly verified).

**Meshy (meshy‑7.1, `latest`).** Verdict: **not worth it; Inferred, and Meshy's own marketing agrees.** Meshy documents no pixel cap for image-to-3D at all, only file size — **20 MB** in the web app (PNG/JPG/JPEG/WebP) and **100 MB** for the API, the latter stated only in a tutorial and not on the API reference (Documented, snippets; [help 9996860](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/9996860-how-to-use-meshy-image-to-3d); [API quickstart tutorial](https://www.meshy.ai/tutorials/api-quickstart-image-to-3d)). Guidance is "≥512×512" (docs), "1024 × 1024 px or higher is ideal" (help), "short side ≥1024; 2048 or higher if you plan to use Refine" (tutorial), and Meshy's image-to-STL page says outright that "very large files don't improve quality, as a sharp, well-framed image at moderate resolution works best" ([docs](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/webapp/image-to-3d); [STL page](https://www.meshy.ai/3d-tools/file-converter/image/to/stl)). The "Image Enhancement" On/Off pre-processing toggle, whose Off setting is "best for high-quality inputs", implies a working resolution that low-res inputs fall below but gives no number (Documented, snippet; Inferred interpretation). Meshy 7.1's "Ultra 4K" raises *output geometry* generation from 2048³ to 4096³ and is single-view only; it is not an input resolution and must not be read as one (press/third-party; [neural4d](https://blog.neural4d.com/comparisons/meshy-7-release-date-and-alternative/); [Webull/PRN](https://www.webull.com/news/15616957784278016)). The one hands-on comparison found (anime character, Meshy multi-view vs single image) saw single-image output "averaging" rivets and decals into the base surface while multi-view "kept those details standing off the surface" (Observed in a demonstration; [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/i-ran-the-same-anime-character-through-meshy-multiview-and-single-image-3d)); no comparison of one large image against several smaller ones exists for any vendor.

**Local Hunyuan3D‑2.x / TRELLIS / TRELLIS.2 / Pixal3D in ComfyUI on the RTX 5070 Ti.** Verdict: **definitely not; Documented from source.** Hunyuan3D‑2.0 and 2.1 run `ImageProcessorV2(size=512, border_ratio=0.15)`: crop to the mask bbox, scale the long side to `int(512×0.85)=435` px, centre on a white 512² canvas, then the DINO encoder resizes to **518** (2.0: DINOv2‑Giant per the paper; 2.1: `facebook/dinov2-large`, `image_size: 518` in the training config), so the robot spans roughly **440 px of a 518² input** and anything finer than ~1/440 of its long dimension is gone before the DiT sees it ([preprocessors.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2/main/hy3dgen/shapegen/preprocessors.py); [2.1 config](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1/main/hy3dshape/configs/hunyuandit-finetuning-flowmatching-dinol518-bf16-lr1e5-4096.yaml); [conditioner.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2/main/hy3dgen/shapegen/models/conditioner.py)). The Hunyuan3D‑Paint texture reference is resized to **512×512** in both 2.0 and 2.1 and the multi-view texture images are generated at 512², then (2.1) upscaled ×4 by RealESRGAN, rendered at 2048 and baked to 4096 — a 4K texture that is super-resolved synthesis, not a projection of your 4K pixels ([multiview_utils.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2/main/hy3dgen/texgen/utils/multiview_utils.py); [textureGenPipeline.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1/main/hy3dpaint/textureGenPipeline.py)). A RunComfy sentence claiming "a larger reference image improves texture quality" is unverified vendor copy contradicted by that code. TRELLIS crops 1.2× the bbox and resizes to **518²** LANCZOS ([trellis_image_to_3d.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/microsoft/TRELLIS/main/trellis/pipelines/trellis_image_to_3d.py)); TRELLIS.2 downscales so the long side is ≤1024 (`scale = min(1, 1024/max_size)`), crops to the alpha bbox and conditions at **512 and 1024** ([trellis2_image_to_3d.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2/main/trellis2/pipelines/trellis2_image_to_3d.py)); ComfyUI's native node does the same at `global_512`/`global_1024` ([nodes_trellis2.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/master/comfy_extras/nodes_trellis2.py)). Pixal3D's **multi-view** path is the one open pipeline where views "are never cropped or rescaled, so framing must match the camera parameters" — the natural consumer of a carefully produced ortho view set rather than a stitched sheet ([Pixal3D README](https://github.com/TencentARC/Pixal3D)). A stitched multi-region master fed as a single image is the **worst case**: the recenter/bbox logic treats everything inside one bounding box as one object, so each region ends up tiny after the 512/518 resize (Inferred from the recenter code).

Two consequences follow. First, the useful input ceiling for geometry is roughly **600–700 px on the object's long side** for Hunyuan3D‑2.x and TRELLIS and about **1024 px** for TRELLIS.2/Pixal3D (Inferred from the constants); for hosted Tripo/Meshy/Hunyuan 3.x no ceiling is knowable, and the shortest test to find it is the resolution sweep in Section 7. Second, the way to "spend" a high-resolution master is to split it into clean orthographic views, each contributing its own 518² or 1024² of information with per-view embeddings (Hunyuan3D‑2mv concatenates `num_views × tokens`; [nodes_hunyuan3d.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/master/comfy_extras/nodes_hunyuan3d.py)), and to keep the full-resolution master for your own Blender bake of albedo detail onto clean UVs after reconstruction, which no generator pipeline will do for you (Inferred).

## 4. Three viable BMe pipeline options

All three options share one spine the evidence insists on: a material-neutral, consistent turnaround is the real input; one master body is reconstructed or modelled first and segmented into the pre-decided part list; every topology, UV, bake and rig decision that touches a deforming region is made by hand in Blender; and the frosted shell is authored opaque-first for Babylon. No route produces a design-faithful, modular, deform-ready character without a human topology pass — Tripo's own blog, the polycount cleanup thread, vendor cleanup guides and a hobbyist running Hunyuan3D locally all report the mandatory cleanup pass (Observed; [polycount](https://polycount.com/discussion/238705/how-are-you-actually-handling-ai-generated-mesh-cleanup-before-engine-handoff); [tspi.at](https://www.tspi.at/2026/05/01/opensource3dassets.html)). The saving from AI is volume discovery and a texture starting point, not topology (Inferred).

### Option A: Cloud reconstruction with multi-view input, then Blender finish

Generate a material-neutral four-view set (the frosted shell rendered as opaque matte mid-grey, internals removed, no rim light, no emissive sole, soft two-sided fill, plain background — each item addresses a documented failure mode: hard shadows become geometry, hotspots and reflections become albedo, glass is "seen through" ([Sloyd](https://www.sloyd.ai/blog/preparing-flat-images-for-better-3d-results); [PrintPal](https://printpal.io/wiki/image-to-3d-best-practices))). Hand-correct the views in 2D for proportion consistency, since Scenario and Tripo both warn that inconsistent views distort the result ([Scenario](https://help.scenario.com/en/articles/use-multi-view-for-better-3d-models)). Submit to Tripo `multiview_to_model` (H3.1, front mandatory, 2–4 views, 1–2K square crops) or Meshy `multi-image-to-3d` (7.1, 1–4 images, first = front); as a variant, submit up to eight views to Hunyuan 3.1 Pro through 3D AI Studio. Run Tripo Segmentation v2 (`granularity`, `split_by_connectivity`, optional `ref_image`) or Smart Segment to cut the master body into named parts ([mesh-segment, excerpt](https://developers.tripo3d.com/en/docs/mesh-segment.md); [TripoSmartSegmentNode, excerpt](https://docs.comfy.org/built-in-nodes/TripoSmartSegmentNode)). In Blender: audit and repair, Quad Remesher or hand retopo at shoulders/elbows/wrists/faceplate rim, UVs, cage bake of the generated albedo onto the clean UVs ([CSM Academy](https://www.csm.ai/academy/retopology-and-baking-textures)), author roughness/metallic/emissive by hand per variant, rig once with Auto-Rig Pro or Rigify, transfer weights to variant parts, export GLB.

### Option C: Blender-native hard-surface modelling with agent-assisted scripting, AI as reference only

Produce the turnaround and, optionally, one quick cloud or local reconstruction used purely as a blockout and proportion check (one large single image is adequate for this use; Inferred). Model the shell, faceplate, limb capsules, socket rings and sneaker from primitives, booleans, bevels and mirror — the sub-problem where reviewers say LLM-scripted Blender does well ("geometry with clear right angles… because primitives and booleans are the right tools") and where it "struggles with organic or character work" (Observed, blogs; [MindStudio](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/claude-blender-mcp-real-world-performance)). Use an agent (ahujasid/blender‑mcp with its `look` tool for viewport/wireframe/X-ray self-inspection, or Mixar's MCP bridge) for the repeatable parts: mesh audit and repair scripts, naming conventions, decimation to budget, cage bakes, weight transfer from the master body, batch export of every variant combination, validator runs ([blender-mcp README](https://github.com/ahujasid/blender-mcp)). Sculpt the organic silicone sleeve by hand; sculpt mode is reported unreachable via MCP and a weight-painted, IK-rigged character "is not realistic with current MCP capabilities" (Claimed, secondary).

Across the three, the controllability ranking is C > A (multi-view) > B/A single-image, and the only documented ways to push AI generation toward fixed proportions are multi-view input from one consistent turnaround and Hunyuan3D‑Omni's bounding-box and skeletal-pose conditioning (~10 GB, shape only, Hunyuan licence) ([Hunyuan3D‑Omni](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-Omni)). Nothing in any document addresses making *independently generated* variant parts share dimensions, which is why variant shells should be derived from the same master part by re-sculpting, shrinkwrapping or re-skinning rather than regenerated — the Ready Player Me "keep the template body and armature, replace surface geometry" pattern ([RPM outfits, excerpt](https://docs.readyplayer.me/ready-player-me/customizing-guides/create-custom-outfits)).

## 5. Recommended small evaluation

Four assets exercise every risk without expanding the project: **one hairless base body** (the master, in a single A-pose), **one interchangeable arm treatment** (a second arm variant built inside the master arm's bounds), **one blank T-shirt** and **one generic sneaker** with an emissive sole. The criteria below are proposals derived from the sourced conventions; the numeric tolerances are **Inferred working assumptions to be tuned in the first pass, not hard limits**, and the only externally sourced figures are Ready Player Me's per-slot caps.

## 8. Concise handoff report

**Project and constraints.** BMe is a stylized robot avatar — frosted translucent shell over visible metallic internals, faceplate head with flush ear panels, interchangeable arm treatments (robotic, silicone, frosted), blank T-shirt, generic sneaker with glowing sole — destined for Babylon.js in browsers and on phones. Production hardware is a Windows PC with an RTX 5070 Ti (16 GB, Blackwell sm_120), 64 GB RAM and ComfyUI. The user is a 30-year 3D professional new to the AI image-to-3D ecosystem; do not redesign the character or expand scope. Research date 2026‑10‑10; versions: Tripo H3.1 `v3.1-20260211` / P1 `P1-20260311`, Meshy 7.1, Hunyuan3D‑2.0/2mv/2.1 open and 3.0/3.1 hosted-only, TRELLIS.2‑4B, Pixal3D (multi-view Sep 2026), ComfyUI v0.34.0 (2026‑08‑25), Babylon.js 9.30.0 (2026‑10‑08), Blender 5.2 LTS (`v5.2.0` 2026‑07‑13), glTF add-on `main` 5.4.4 (targets Blender ≥ 5.3, in development; the 5.2-bundled branch was not read), Mixar v4.2.3 (2026‑10‑10). Nothing was tested and no video was watched; labels are Documented / Observed in a demonstration / Inferred / Unverified; many vendor facts are indexed snippets of unopened pages.

**Decisions the evidence supports.** Do not produce the 4K+ stitched master as converter input. Produce instead one **material-neutral, consistent four-view turnaround** (shell as opaque matte mid-grey, internals removed, no rim or emissive light, plain background), each view a tight ~1–2K square crop, and feed views, not a sheet: Tripo `multiview_to_model` (front mandatory, 2–4 views), Meshy multi-image (1–4, first = front), Hunyuan 3.1 Pro via 3D AI Studio (up to 8 views), Hunyuan3D‑2mv or Pixal3D multi-view locally. Open-model conditioning is 518² (Hunyuan3D‑2.x, TRELLIS), 512 + 1024 (TRELLIS.2), Paint reference 512² — code-verified — and hosted conditioning is undocumented and must not be inferred from these. Keep upload caps (Tripo 20 MB JPEG/PNG; Alibaba-hosted Tripo 20–6000 px/side; Meshy 20 MB web / 100 MB API per a tutorial; 3D AI Studio <50 MB) separate from processing, output texture (Tripo 2K/4K/8K tiers, Meshy 2k/4k/8k, Hunyuan3D‑2.1 bake 4096²) and geometric resolution (Hunyuan3D octree 384 default / ComfyUI 256 default 512 max; TRELLIS.2 512³–1536³; Meshy Ultra 4096³). Reconstruct **one master body**, segment into the pre-decided part list (head, faceplate, torso shell, pelvis, 2× upper arm, forearm, hand, thigh, shin, foot, T-shirt, sneaker), and derive variant shells from the same master part; author hidden sockets and seam rings by hand. Retopologise anything that deforms (Quad Remesher or hand loops at shoulders/elbows/wrists/faceplate rim), bake only the AI albedo onto clean UVs, author roughness/metallic/emissive per variant so variants are texture-driven, rig once (ARP or Rigify, deform bones only, humanoid names), transfer weights to variant parts. Export per part as GLB skinned to the full armature; T-shirt and sneaker colourways as `KHR_materials_variants` from Blender's Variants tab; run gltf-transform or gltfpack for KTX2 (ETC1S albedo, UASTC normal/ORM) and meshopt; self-host the KTX2/Draco/meshopt decoders; validate in the Babylon Sandbox with the project `.env`. Author the frosted shell **opaque-first** (internals baked into albedo + emissive) because Babylon's glTF loader spins up a `TransmissionHelper` that renders every opaque mesh into a second 1024², 4× MSAA, half-float, mipmapped target for any transmissive or diffuse-transmissive material; keep real transmission as a measured upgrade tier. Emissive sole via `KHR_materials_emissive_strength` plus a `GlowLayer` restricted to the sole at a fixed 256–512 target. Budgets are working assumptions: 15–40k triangles assembled, one material per swappable part, 1K textures, ≤4 weights/vertex; the only sourced caps are Ready Player Me's (outfit 20,000; top 6,000; footwear 2,000; body 2,000). Default to the WebGL2 `Engine`, opt into `WebGPUEngine` behind a feature check; Babylon provides no automatic fallback.

**Local-first status.** On the 5070 Ti, shape generation is realistic (Hunyuan3D‑2.0/2mv ~6 GB, 2.1 ~10 GB; TRELLIS.2/Pixal3D native at 32³/1024 per community reports); PBR texturing is **not** a solved step: ComfyUI's native Hunyuan3D nodes are shape-only, wrappers need a compiled `custom_rasterizer` with no sm_120 Windows wheel found (kijai #203, #214 open), Hunyuan3D‑2.1 texturing is 21 GB official, and TRELLIS.2's 16 GB fit is untested here. Hunyuan3D 2.x/Part/Omni licences exclude the EU/UK/South Korea; TRELLIS.2, Pixal3D, PartCrafter, Hi3DGen are MIT; PartPacker is non-commercial.

## Conclusion

The research reframes the original question. The project does not have an input-resolution problem; it has an information-routing problem. Every converter whose internals can be read spends a fixed, small budget of pixels per view — 518² or 1024² — and the hosted products give no reason to believe they are different, so detail gets into the model through the number and consistency of views, and into the final asset through your own bake from the master onto hand-made UVs. That moves the master image from "converter input" to "source of truth for crops and texture", which is a cheaper and more controllable role, and it makes the four-view turnaround, not the 8K sheet, the artefact worth perfecting.

The second shift is about where the risk sits. The modelling, UV, bake and rig work is the designer's home ground and the evidence says it stays manual at the joints regardless of route; the genuinely uncertain items are infrastructural and cheap to test — a 30-minute compile check decides whether local texturing exists, a ~100-credit sweep decides whether hosted inputs above 2K matter, and one profiler run on a phone decides whether the frosted shell can afford real transmission. Agent tooling, whether blender-mcp or Mixar, earns its place as a scripting and batch-validation assistant for the repeatable tail of that work, with Mixar's hosted architecture and unverified terms as the price of its convenience.
